SOME WORDS FROM MY HEART

A Letter Just for You.

There are things that are easier to write than to say. So take your time and read this whenever you feel like it.

OPEN WHEN YOU'RE READY ♡

Hey, bestie...

I don't know how to explain everything perfectly, but I want you to know that you really matter to me. There is something I have wanted you to understand about our friendship.

First of all, thank you for being a part of my life. Thank you for the moments we've shared, the laughs, the conversations, and even the times when we didn't completely understand each other. All of it has become part of our story, and I value that.

I want you to remember one thing: whatever situation you are in, you can talk to me. If something is bothering you, if you're confused, if you're sad, or if something has happened and you don't know what to do, please don't feel like you have to handle everything alone.

I may not be able to solve every problem, and I might not always give the perfect advice. But I'll try to listen and understand you. Sometimes, you don't need someone to fix everything. You just need someone you can talk to without feeling judged.

And there is one more thing I really want to say. Please don't hide the truth from me just because you're scared of how I might react. I know I might get upset about something sometimes, and I might need a little time to understand it. I'm not saying I'll react perfectly every time.

But I would rather we speak honestly and work through something difficult than have you feel you need to lie to me or hide something just to keep our friendship okay. Your feelings matter, and so does the trust between us.

I want to be a friend you can talk to freely, not someone you feel afraid of. You can tell me when something is wrong, even if it is difficult to say. We might not agree about everything, but we can still try to understand each other.

I hope that, even as we grow older and life gets busier, we continue to make space for honest conversations, little jokes, random updates, and the kind of friendship where we can be ourselves.

You don't have to pretend to be strong every day. You don't have to have everything figured out. And you don't have to find the perfect words before talking to me.

Thank you for being you. I hope you always have reasons to smile, people who genuinely care about you, and the courage to speak up whenever something is troubling you.

Whatever life brings, I hope we keep treating each other with honesty, patience, and kindness. That's what I want from our friendship—not perfection, just something real.

Always your best friend,
Nani ♡
